# Transcribing Moriscas on Trial  

"Transcribing Moriscas on Trial" has multi-year ambitions to create digital tools and publish OERs that advance teaching and scholarship on the morisco experience while equipping undergraduates with the paleographic skills and digital competencies to engage rigorously with these important documents.  

## Goals and objectives  

1. By the end of the grant, to have a proof of concept platform for a digital edition for one to two trials from Penn’s holdings.
2. To create a collaborative transcription project on Zooniverse (or similar platform) that will allow us to finish transcribing Penn’ holdings, standardize transcriptions, and review work for this and future projects.
3. Finish a first-pass transcription of the remaining 430 images held at Penn, standardize all transcriptions on the new platform, and modernize the text of key trials for classroom use.
4. Design and tag the metadata for the 950 transcribed images (430 in this round; 520 from prior summer research), beginning with the trials selected for publication in the proof of concept digital edition.
5. If time permits, begin collaborative transcribing the 850 images held in the Spanish archives, initiate collaboration with AHN and design expansion for project in year 2.
6. Explore HTR and machine learning workflows and models for transcription
